# Billing

Billing owns ISP subscriptions, invoice generation, adjustments, customer balances, billing cycles, and the accounting ledger for invoice payments.

Billing persists module records to the shared PostgreSQL database through the `billing_records` JSONB table when `DATABASE_URL` is configured. Financial posting operations use a cross-worker PostgreSQL transaction lock, native invoice/receipt sequences, database uniqueness constraints, and durable `billing_posting_events`. It falls back to in-memory state only when PostgreSQL is not configured.

- Frontend page/styles: `features/billing/web/`
- API router/state: `features/billing/api/billing/`
- API prefix: `/api/billing`

Current scope:

- Customer-linked subscriptions using Customer Profiling customer IDs
- Finance-focused Overview driven by one billing-month/as-of control. Its scorecard shows selected-month net billed value, cash collected, cash collection rate, all-open A/R, and overdue A/R without mixing unrelated current-month and all-time metrics. Monthly collection performance, automatic-billing health, posting reconciliation, and compact exception counts remain summary-only; no customer table or A/R aging panel is rendered on Overview.
- The Overview `Billing Control Center` links `Installation Fee Pending` and `Ready for Monthly Billing` to the matching Subscriptions queue, missing invoice cycles and scheduler/run exceptions to Billing Runs, and accounts requiring follow-up to the dedicated Collections tab.
- Service Account installation fee decision workflow before monthly subscription billing starts. Billing can charge a one-time installation invoice, record a promo/approved waiver, or record that no installation fee is required. The charge form defaults `Standard Installation Fee` and `Amount to Bill Customer` to PHP 1,500 when Service has no configured install fee, and both values are editable.
- The Subscriptions tab owns the filtered Billing Setup Queue table. Pending accounts expose `Resolve Fee`; resolved but unbilled accounts expose `Start Billing` to populate customer, plan, service reference, catalog snapshot, billing mode, billing start date, and catalog list price.
- First-cycle prepaid proration for Service Account billing. When a prepaid Service Account starts mid-month, Billing automatically creates the first invoice for the activation/start date through the calendar month end, rounds the prorated amount up to the next whole peso, records the generation business date as the issue date, keeps payment due no earlier than service start, and moves the next invoice date to the first day of the next full month.
- First-cycle postpaid proration for Service Account billing. When a postpaid Service Account starts mid-month, Billing automatically creates the first invoice for the activation/start date through the calendar month end, charges only active days rounded up to the next whole peso, records the generation business date as the issue date, applies payment terms from the later of period close or issue date, and moves the next invoice date to the first day of the next full month.
- Billing-owned searchable Promotions catalog for generic promo setup, date windows, discount type, payment condition, priority, optional billing-mode restriction, stacking policy, and audit-friendly promo codes. Promo Code is optional; Billing auto-generates an internal code when it is left blank. Customer and plan targets are not stored on promotion definitions; monthly eligibility is assigned explicitly on each subscription.
- PREPAID and POSTPAID subscriptions can qualify for one or more monthly-service promotions, including an Early Bird promotion plus another qualified offer. More than one promotion is allowed only when every selected rule is marked stackable. Full monthly invoices snapshot the qualified promotion terms, and Billing applies them in priority order against the remaining invoice balance.
- Catalog-controlled subscription pricing for Service-linked subscriptions, with explicit price override amount and reason when the billable price differs from Service Catalog
- Manual/legacy subscription entry for cases that do not yet have a Service Account
- Monthly prepaid and postpaid subscription modes
- Durable automatic monthly billing runs. The API worker checks every five minutes using the configured business timezone, generates prepaid invoices seven days before the cycle by default, generates postpaid invoices at calendar-month close, records the actual generation business date as `issueDate`, and catches up missed cycles in order without creating a due date before issuance.
- Billing Runs operations tab with the current due-cycle preview, estimated billing value, scheduler health, manual `Run now`, durable run history, per-subscription outcomes, and retained exceptions.
- Installation fee decision records with one-time invoice creation for charged fees
- Invoice creation, draft editing, immutable posting, voiding, line items, due dates, status derivation, service references, and duplicate-safe monthly subscription invoice generation. Manual and automatic invoice summaries derive `billingPeriodMonth` and `billingPeriodLabel` from their cycle dates. Monthly charge descriptions show that customer-facing period in parentheses, such as `Fiber 100 monthly internet service (August 2026)`, instead of placing the internal service ID in the description. New invoices persist the period-based text; legacy posted invoices receive the same non-mutating detail/PDF projection. The Invoices tab provides instant search and keeps its ledger compact with only customer, billing period, balance, and actions. The ledger paginates locally at 20 rows by default, supports 10/20/50-row page sizes, and resets to the first page when search or page size changes without altering the list API used by Billing, POS, Collector, and Customer Profiling. `View` opens the authoritative invoice detail with service, line items, financial activity, totals, notes, and audit state; `Download PDF` returns the same ledger projection as a paginated billing statement.
- Immutable account-summary snapshots captured when an invoice is issued. Each snapshot records prior open invoice references and issue-time balances, payment/credit/debit activity since the previous invoice, the current invoice balance, and the total account amount due. For readability, invoice detail and PDF statements present only `Total Account Amount Due`, followed by Previous Unpaid Invoices and the current Charges; the additional snapshot fields remain available for audit but are not repeated in the document. Prior balances are never added to the current invoice's charges or ledger balance. Existing invoices created before this contract remain valid without a reconstructed snapshot.
- Payment API ledger for invoice-level, customer-level, and advance-credit payments. `POST /api/billing/payments` requires an `Idempotency-Key` header and accepts invoice `allocations`, optional `advanceAmount`, allocation-level `promotionIds` bundles, quote date/fingerprint fields, and legacy singular `promotionId` fields. Billing revalidates the bundle and quote against the current balance, requires the allocation to match the combined discounted payable, and atomically posts one immutable `PAYMENT_PROMOTION` credit adjustment per promotion. Advance is allowed only after every current invoice is fully settled by actual allocation plus any validated promotion credit, so a promoted payoff and excess advance can safely coexist on one receipt. Customer-facing invoice payment posting is handled in Point of Sale -> Invoice Payments and Collector.
- Multi-customer outage rebates from the Adjustments tab. Staff select the outage start/end datetimes and one or more active subscriber accounts; the amount is not manually entered. `POST /api/billing/adjustments/outage-rebates/preview` prorates each active subscription's monthly recurring charge by the exact affected hours in each calendar month, correctly splits cross-month windows, combines multiple plans per customer, and previews how much can be applied now versus carried forward. `POST /api/billing/adjustments/outage-rebates` requires an `Idempotency-Key` and the current preview fingerprint, then atomically posts the full calculated amount as one immutable customer-level `SERVICE_REBATE` credit per selected customer. An open service bill consumes the credit immediately up to its balance; any remainder, or the full amount when no bill is open, stays available and is automatically applied to the next generated monthly invoice. Duplicate customer/window rebates, stale previews, future outage ends, partial batches, and customers without an active priced subscription are rejected. Each credit retains the outage window, timezone, duration, plan/rate calculation inputs, batch ID, operator, initial application, and carry-forward amounts for audit.
- Immutable posted adjustments with idempotent creation and reversal/void handling for invoice credits and debits. The invoice-specific API contract remains available for controlled accounting corrections and system-generated promotion credits.
- Customer balance summary with outstanding, credit, overdue, open invoice counts, and Monthly Aging text that combines unpaid monthly invoice periods with expected-but-uninvoiced service periods
- Overview-level Monthly Collection Performance for Finance. `GET /api/billing/collection-performance` accepts billing month, as-of date, payment status, search, page, and page size filters. The monthly cohort includes only issued monthly-service invoice types and counts each billed customer once, even when that customer has several service invoices. It reports fully paid, partially paid, and unpaid subscriber counts; subscriber settlement and cash collection rates; gross charges; direct invoice credits; net billed value; posted cash allocations; applied account credits; rebates; outstanding value; and posting reconciliation variance. Rebate, promotion, and account-credit value can settle a subscriber but is never counted as cash.
- The same report independently reconstructs all open invoice receivables as of the selected date and returns open/overdue amounts, invoice/customer counts, oldest delinquency, and `Current`, `1-30`, `31-60`, `61-90`, and `90+` aging buckets. These totals include valid invoice types outside the selected monthly-service cohort and therefore represent the A/R position rather than only monthly subscriber performance.
- The dedicated Collections tab owns the operational Collection Worklist for the selected billing month. It defaults to `ACTION_REQUIRED`, combining partially paid and unpaid subscribers, and supports server-side status filtering, customer/account/invoice search, and pagination. Rows are ranked with overdue accounts first and show customer, payment status, due/overdue context, monthly invoice count and net billed amount, cash and account-credit settlement, rebates, and remaining balance. Clicking Partially Paid or Unpaid on Overview opens the matching Collections filter. Fully paid customer detail is intentionally omitted. The former Recent Invoices, Collection Risk, and Accounts Requiring Collection tables remain removed.
- Customer avatar behavior using System Settings `CustomerEmotionAvatar`; overdue balances and open invoices can push the visible mood toward warning or angry.

Financial posting integrity controls:

- PostgreSQL is reloaded as the source of truth at each read snapshot and before each mutation.
- Billing mutations serialize across API workers with a transaction-scoped advisory lock and commit all affected records and posting events together.
- Native PostgreSQL sequences issue non-reusable `INV-YYYYMM-######` and `OR-YYYYMM-######` numbers.
- Database indexes enforce unique document numbers, idempotency keys per record type, and one invoice per subscription billing-cycle start.
- Automatic and manual runs use durable `billing_run` records. Invoice posting remains transaction-safe per cycle, daily automatic runs reuse one idempotency key, and every generated cycle uses the existing unique subscription/cycle posting key.
- Manual invoice, payment, and adjustment creation requires `Idempotency-Key`; a matching retry replays the original record and a mismatched retry returns HTTP 409.
- Posted invoices, payments, and adjustments cannot be edited. Recurring invoice corrections use credit/debit adjustments, installation invoices are reversed through their fee decision, and direct invoice void is limited to manual invoices. Payment/adjustment reversals and voided manual invoices remain visible with actor, timestamp, and reason metadata.
- The issue-time `accountSummaryAtIssue` projection is written once at posting and is not recalculated after later payments or adjustments. Prior invoices remain separate open items; `totalAccountAmountDue` is a historical presentation total, not a carried-forward line item.
- Advance amounts remain on their immutable source receipt, and rebates remain on their immutable adjustment record. Current and future monthly invoices consume those sources through separate auditable `credit_application` records. A source payment or rebate adjustment cannot be voided after any amount has been applied.
- Monthly collection reporting reconstructs invoice, receipt-allocation, adjustment, and credit-application activity at the selected as-of date using actual posting timestamps. Backdated payment business dates cannot make a receipt appear before it was posted, and voided records remain historically active only before their void timestamp.
- Collector aging includes a versioned, day-bound `promotionQuote` per open invoice plus account-level regular balance, automatic savings, and discounted `payableToday`. The quote fingerprint covers the invoice balance, payment date, promotion bundle, discount, and net payable; stale Collector submissions fail with HTTP 409 before posting.
- Focused tests live in `features/billing/api/tests/test_financial_integrity.py` and run with `python3 -m unittest discover -s features/billing/api/tests -p 'test_*.py' -v`.

Demo Billing seed records are disabled by default. Set `BILLING_SEED_DEMO=true` only for disposable demo environments.

Automatic biller environment:

- `BILLING_AUTO_BILLER_ENABLED` enables the API lifecycle worker; defaults to `true`.
- `BILLING_TIMEZONE` controls the billing business date; defaults to `Asia/Manila`.
- `BILLING_PREPAID_LEAD_DAYS` controls how many days before a prepaid cycle its invoice is generated; defaults to `7`.
- `BILLING_SCHEDULER_INTERVAL_SECONDS` controls reconciliation frequency; defaults to `300` and is bounded to 30-86400 seconds.
